# 13_DIGITAL_CATALOG_AND_QR.md — Unified Digital Catalog & Product QR Kit

## 1. Single Source of Truth: Product Registry
- Exhibitors never build a catalog twice.
- The **Digital Catalog** (`/s/{showroomSlug}/catalog`) is automatically populated in real time directly from the project's Product Registry.

## 2. Product QR Kit
- Every registered product receives a persistent, trackable QR code.
- Public URL structure: `/s/{showroomSlug}/p/{productSlug}`.
- Allows on-site exhibition attendees to scan physical equipment in the hall and instantly launch the 3D digital specification card and request wholesale RFQs.
