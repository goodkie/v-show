## 📸 HOW TO PHOTOGRAPH YOUR BOOTH
*Simple guidelines to create the best interactive digital showroom.*

---

### Option 1: BEST & FASTEST — 360° Camera
*Recommended for: Photo Immersive Booth (Full 360° Exploration)*

1. **Equipment**: Insta360 X3/X4, Ricoh Theta Z1/X, GoPro MAX, or professional Matterport Pro2/Pro3.
2. **Placement**: Stand in the center of your booth aisle or main equipment area at eye level (approx. 1.5m / 5ft height).
3. **Capture**: Take 1 to 3 panoramas from distinct vantage points (e.g., Booth Center, Left Workstation, Right Product Cell).
4. **Export**: Export as standard high-resolution JPEG (`8192 × 4096` or `5760 × 2880`).

---

### Option 2: ACCESSIBLE — Smartphone or Digital Camera
*Recommended for: Multi-View Photo Booth or Single Photo Showroom*

1. **Overview Shot**: Stand 3–5 meters back from your booth and take a clear, horizontal (landscape) wide shot of the entire stand.
2. **Product Detail Shots**: Take clear, well-lit photos of individual machinery, products, and demonstration counters.
3. **Consistency**:
   - Keep the same camera orientation (Landscape recommended).
   - Ensure good exhibition hall lighting and minimize foreground foot traffic.
4. **Result**: Your photos will instantly generate a crisp **Multi-View Photo Showroom** with interactive product drawers and buyer RFQ tools!
