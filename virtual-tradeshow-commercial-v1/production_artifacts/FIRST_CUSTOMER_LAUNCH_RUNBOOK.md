# FIRST CUSTOMER LAUNCH RUNBOOK
**vivPR V-Show — Step-by-Step Operator Runbook**

---

## 1. Pilot Customer Recruitment & Onboarding
1. **Outreach:** Send cold email or invitation using `first_customer_launch/02_FREE_PILOT_INVITATION_EMAIL.md`.
2. **Intake:** Direct prospect to `https://v-show-commercial-v1-production.up.railway.app/start.html`.
3. **Qualification:** Open `/grand-control.html` -> Sales Pipeline -> Review lead -> Click "Convert to Pilot".
4. **Account Delivery:** Provide the 16+ char temporary password to the exhibitor admin.
5. **Photo Submission:** Exhibitor uploads 60–100 photos according to `first_customer_launch/07_BOOTH_CAPTURE_GUIDE.md`.
6. **Capture QA:** Run pre-flight QA (`QA_PASSED`).
7. **GPU Compute:** Platform Owner approves reconstruction in Grand Control.
8. **Booth Setup:** Exhibitor adds up to 5 products and 3 hotspots.
9. **Publish:** Set booth to published in Exhibitor Console.
10. **Telemetry & Value:** Monitor visits, hotspot clicks, and RFQs.
