# 07. 3D RECONSTRUCTION & COMPUTATIONAL DISCLOSURE
**vivPR V-Show — Technology Disclaimers**

---

## 1. Technical Pipeline
Customer Booth Photographs (60–100) ➔ Structure-from-Motion (COLMAP) ➔ 3D Gaussian Splatting (Nerfstudio / Splatfacto) ➔ Radiance Field Splat Matrix (.SPZ / .PLY) ➔ Spark WebGL Client Rendering.

---

## 2. Mandatory Disclaimers
1. **Computational Approximation:** The virtual booth is a mathematical approximation of optical radiance and may contain rendering artifacts, floaters, or minor texture blurring.
2. **Not a CAD Tool:** The 3D scene is for marketing visualization and must not be used as architectural, structural, or sub-millimeter manufacturing specifications.
3. **No AI Model Pre-Training on Customer Data:** Customer booth photos are used solely to reconstruct the specific customer's 3D booth and are never aggregated or used to train foundational AI models without permission.
