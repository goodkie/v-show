## 2. Platform Architecture & Service Description

### User Roles & Tenancy
1. **Exhibitor Commercial Tenants:** Brand manufacturers and distributors who upload trade show booth photographs, product metadata, and media to generate interactive 3D virtual showrooms.
2. **Event Organizers:** Trade show management companies overseeing multi-booth digital exhibition pavilions.
3. **Public / Commercial Buyer Visitors:** Global business attendees browsing virtual booths, engaging with 3D product hotspots, submitting formal Requests for Quotations (RFQs), requesting sample products, and booking appointments.
4. **Platform Owners (vivPR Operations):** Administrative team managing infrastructure, reconstruction quality assurance, and commercial billing.
