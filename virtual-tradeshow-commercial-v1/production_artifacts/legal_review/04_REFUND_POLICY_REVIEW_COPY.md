## 1. Current Refund Provisions
- **7-Day Initial Subscription Refund Window:** New subscribers on paid plans (PRO $299 / BUSINESS $799) may request a full refund within 7 calendar days of first charge, provided no GPU reconstruction was initiated.
- **Completed 3D Reconstruction Exception:** Because 3D Gaussian Splatting consumes substantial non-recoverable GPU cloud computing resources, once a reconstruction job is approved and executed, the setup/reconstruction allocation is non-refundable.
- **Mid-Cycle Cancellation:** Customers may cancel anytime via the self-serve Stripe Customer Portal. Access remains active through the end of the paid billing period with zero prorated refunds for partial months.
