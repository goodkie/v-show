# dn’a-C09.18 — Cancellation & Reactivation

## Cancellation Policy (`POST_CANCELLATION_BOOTH_POLICY`)
- User cancels subscription → sets `cancelAtPeriodEnd = true` (`CANCEL_AT_PERIOD_END`).
- Access and public booth remain live until current period end date.
- Post-expiry: Public exhibition listing pauses; studio booth data and product assets are preserved indefinitely.
- Reactivation: Customer can resume subscription at any time prior to or after period end without recreating their booth.
