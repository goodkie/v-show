# OUTREACH SCORECARD SPECIFICATION
**vivPR V-Show — KPI Accounting Standards**

---

## 1. Measured Metrics
- **Sprint Capacity:** Target 10 Real Prospects (`X / 10`)
- **Prospects Contacted:** Total unique prospects sent outreach.
- **Reply Rate:** $\frac{\text{Replies}}{\text{Contacted}}$ (Safely renders `N/A` if Contacted $= 0$).
- **Positive Reply Rate:** $\frac{\text{Positive Replies}}{\text{Contacted}}$.
- **Demo Rate:** $\frac{\text{Demos Scheduled}}{\text{Contacted}}$.
- **Pilot Acceptance Rate:** $\frac{\text{Pilots Accepted}}{\text{Contacted}}$.

---

- `REAL Paid Customers = 0`
- `REAL MRR = $0.00`
- `REAL ARR = $0.00`
