## 1. 16-Step Commercial Lifecycle
1. **Public Discovery:** Prospect visits `https://v-show-commercial-v1-production.up.railway.app/`.
2. **Interactive Demo:** Prospect explores `/demo.html` to evaluate 3D radiance fidelity.
3. **Application Submission:** Prospect completes `/start.html` with event and booth metadata.
4. **Lead Qualification:** Platform Owner inspects lead in Grand Control (`/grand-control.html` -> Sales Pipeline).
5. **Pre-Activation Execution:** Platform Owner clicks "Convert to Pilot" (quota: max 1 real customer).
6. **Credential Delivery:** Generated 16+ character temporary password delivered to exhibitor admin.
7. **First Login & Password Reset:** Customer logs into `/admin.html` and sets 12+ char password.
8. **Booth Photography Upload:** Customer uploads 60–100 photos according to `/capture-guide.html`.
9. **Capture QA Validation:** Platform executes pre-flight QA (`QA_PASSED`).
10. **Reconstruction Approval:** Platform Owner approves 3DGS pipeline for GPU compute.
11. **3D Booth Review:** Customer reviews reconstructed 3D showroom in Admin console.
12. **Public Exhibition Publishing:** Booth published to event Lobby (`/lobby.html`).
13. **Buyer Lead & RFQ Engagement:** Real buyers interact with product hotspots and submit RFQs.
14. **Value Milestone Trigger:** Server records `first_lead` and `first_rfq` milestones.
15. **Contextual PRO Recommendation:** System recommends PRO upgrade upon hitting Free tier limits.
16. **Stripe Test Rehearsal:** Customer runs Stripe Test Checkout ($299/mo) with $0.00 real cash impact.
