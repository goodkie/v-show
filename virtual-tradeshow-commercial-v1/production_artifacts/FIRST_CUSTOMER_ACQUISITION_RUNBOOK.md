# FIRST CUSTOMER ACQUISITION RUNBOOK
**vivPR V-Show — Step-by-Step Operator Runbook**

---

## 1. Full Commercial Lifecycle
1. **Discovery:** Prospect visits `https://v-show-commercial-v1-production.up.railway.app/` or `/demo.html`.
2. **Application:** Prospect submits `/pilot-apply.html` with trade show details.
3. **Qualification:** Platform Owner reviews lead in Grand Control -> Sales Pipeline.
4. **Invitation:** Platform Owner clicks "Convert to Pilot" (quota: max 1 real customer).
5. **Account Setup:** Customer admin logs into `/admin.html` with temporary password, resets password (12+ chars).
6. **Capture Intake:** Customer uploads 60–100 photos according to `/capture-guide.html`.
7. **Pre-flight QA:** System validates photo count, duplicate estimate, and resolution.
8. **Reconstruction:** Platform Owner approves 3DGS processing.
9. **Showroom Publish:** Booth published to Exhibition Lobby (`/lobby.html`).
10. **Buyer Traffic:** Real buyers explore products and submit RFQs.
11. **Success Report:** Telemetry tracks Pilot Success Score (0–100).
12. **Upgrade Intent:** Customer requests PRO ($299/mo) with zero real cash charge ($0.00).
