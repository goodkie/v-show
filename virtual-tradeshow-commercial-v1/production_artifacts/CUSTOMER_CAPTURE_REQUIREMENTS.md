# CUSTOMER BOOTH CAPTURE & QA REQUIREMENTS
**Virtual Trade Show Commercial V1 — 3D Photography Standard**

---

## 1. Capture Guidelines
- **Recommended Count:** 60–100 independent multi-angle photographs.
- **Resolution:** 4K (3840x2160) or 2K (1920x1080) minimum.
- **Coverage:** 360-degree perimeter orbits with 60% overlap between adjacent frames.
- **Lighting:** Uniform, diffuse trade-show lighting without heavy dynamic shadows.

---

## 2. Pre-flight QA Criteria
- **Reject:** Contact sheets, duplicates, extreme low-resolution images (< 800px), unsupported MIME formats.
- **Intake States:** `NO_DATA` -> `UPLOADING` -> `QA_PENDING` -> `QA_PASSED` / `QA_FAILED` -> `READY_FOR_RECONSTRUCTION`.
