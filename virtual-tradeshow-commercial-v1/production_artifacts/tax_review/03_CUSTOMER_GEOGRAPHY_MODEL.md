# 03. CUSTOMER GEOGRAPHY & SOURCING MODEL
**vivPR V-Show — Tax Nexus Geographic Distribution**

---

## 1. Projected Customer Footprint
- **In-State (New Jersey):** Domestic exhibitors with physical presence in NJ (Subject to NJ Sales & Use Tax evaluation).
- **Interstate (U.S. Multi-State):** B2B exhibitors across California, Texas, Illinois, New York, Florida (Subject to South Dakota v. Wayfair economic nexus thresholds, typically $100k sales or 200 transactions).
- **International (Cross-Border):** Global trade show exhibitors in EU, UK, Asia (Subject to B2B reverse charge rules with verified VAT/Tax IDs).
