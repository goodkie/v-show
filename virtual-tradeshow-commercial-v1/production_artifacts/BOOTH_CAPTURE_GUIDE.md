# BOOTH CAPTURE GUIDE (OPERATIONAL COPY)
**Virtual Trade Show Commercial V1 — 3D Reconstruction Photography Guidelines**

---

## 1. Quick Capture Checklist
- **Photo Count:** 60 to 100 high-resolution images.
- **Resolution:** 4K (3840x2160) or 2K (1920x1080).
- **Lighting:** Diffuse, uniform exhibit hall lighting.
- **Overlap:** 60% overlap between adjacent shots.

---

## 2. Capture Path
1. **Eye-Level 360 Loop (8 cardinal directions):** Front, Front-Left, Left, Rear-Left, Rear, Rear-Right, Right, Front-Right.
2. **Elevated High-Angle Loop:** Aimed slightly downward.
3. **Low-Angle Floor Loop:** Aimed at product bases.
4. **Flagship Product Detail Passes:** 3–5 close-ups per key product.
