# 07. BOOTH PHOTOGRAPHY CAPTURE GUIDE (CUSTOMER COPY)
**vivPR V-Show — Step-by-Step Photography Instructions**

---

## 1. Golden Rules for 3D Gaussian Splatting
1. **Target:** 60 to 100 high-resolution photos.
2. **Resolution:** 4K (3840x2160) or 2K (1920x1080) minimum.
3. **Lighting:** Keep exhibition lighting steady; avoid flash.
4. **Overlap:** Ensure 60–80% visual overlap between sequential photos.

---

## 2. 5-Pass Capture Sequence
- **Pass 1 (Perimeter Orbit):** 20 photos walking clockwise around the outer booth boundaries.
- **Pass 2 (Eye-Level Mid-Range):** 20 photos capturing the booth interior from 2 meters away.
- **Pass 3 (High & Low Elevation):** 15 photos held high (facing downward) and 15 photos low (facing upward).
- **Pass 4 (Product Detail):** 3–5 dedicated close-ups per major display unit.
- **Pass 5 (Signage & Branding):** Crisp framing of company logo and overhead banners.
