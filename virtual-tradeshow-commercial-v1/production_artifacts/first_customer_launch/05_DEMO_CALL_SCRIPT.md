# 05. 15-MINUTE PROSPECT DEMO CALL SCRIPT
**vivPR V-Show — Commercial Sales Demo Protocol**

---

## 1. Introduction & Discovery (3 mins)
- "Thanks for taking the time, {Name}. Today I want to show you how {CompanyName} can keep generating qualified buyer leads long after {EventName} closes."
- **Key Discovery Questions:**
  - "How many physical products/machines are you displaying in your booth?"
  - "Do you currently have a way for buyers who couldn't attend in person to walk through your booth?"

---

## 2. Interactive Demo Walkthrough (7 mins)
- Open `/demo.html` on screen share.
- Demonstrate 1-finger orbit and 2-finger pinch zoom.
- Click on product hotspot: show specs, brochure download, RFQ form, and sample order buttons.
- Showcase mobile landscape responsiveness.

---

## 3. Pilot Offer & Next Steps (5 mins)
- Explain the 100% Free Pilot terms (zero credit card, zero auto-charge).
- Review photo requirements (60–100 photos using `/capture-guide.html`).
- Provide onboarding credentials link.
