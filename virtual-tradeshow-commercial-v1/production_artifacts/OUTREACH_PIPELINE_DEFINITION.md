# OUTREACH PIPELINE DEFINITION
**vivPR V-Show — Stage Definitions**

---

## 1. Outreach Lifecycle Stages
1. `RESEARCHED`: Prospect profile identified.
2. `READY_TO_CONTACT`: Validated contact email & upcoming trade show.
3. `CONTACTED`: Initial outreach email sent.
4. `REPLIED`: Prospect responded to outreach.
5. `INTERESTED`: Prospect expressed curiosity or requested details.
6. `NOT_INTERESTED`: Prospect declined.
7. `FOLLOW_UP`: Follow-up message sent.
8. `DEMO_PROPOSED`: 15-minute 3D walkthrough proposed.
9. `DEMO_SCHEDULED`: Walkthrough calendar time confirmed.
10. `DEMO_COMPLETED`: Walkthrough delivered.
11. `PILOT_PROPOSED`: Free pilot terms offered.
12. `PILOT_ACCEPTED`: Exhibitor confirmed free pilot participation.
13. `PILOT_DECLINED`: Exhibitor declined free pilot.
14. `NO_RESPONSE`: 2 follow-ups sent with zero response.
