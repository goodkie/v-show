Refund & Cancellation Policy

Policy Version: 2026.1-draft Status: Draft Effective Date: [TO BE COMPLETED BEFORE LIVE BILLING]

1. Subscription Cancellation Policy

Exhibitors may cancel recurring subscription renewals at any time directly through the Stripe Customer Portal inside the Exhibitor Admin Console.

  • Upon cancellation, paid plan entitlements (elevated product limits and precision 3DGS rendering) remain active until the end of the current paid billing cycle.
  • No further recurring charges will be incurred. At cycle expiration, the account will safely transition to the Free tier.
  • Cancellation does not delete existing product listings, uploaded catalogs, or collected buyer leads.

2. Refund Evaluation Criteria

As a SaaS platform providing instantaneous cloud infrastructure and specialized GPU rendering services, refunds are handled according to the following fair principles:

  • Initial 7-Day Window (GPU Unused): Within 7 days of the initial subscription charge, if no 3D Gaussian Splatting reconstruction jobs have been executed, a full 100% refund is available upon request.
  • Post-GPU Execution: Once cloud GPU compute resources have been expended to process 3DGS neural reconstructions, monthly subscription fees become non-refundable for that billing cycle due to irrevocable computing costs.
  • System & Billing Errors: Any duplicate charges or billing system errors will be investigated immediately and refunded 100% without delay.
  • Service Outages: Continuous verified platform outages exceeding 24 hours will be compensated with proportional billing credits or prorated refunds.

3. Refund Request Procedure

To request a refund, submit a support message through the Admin Console or contact our billing desk with your invoice number. Requests are reviewed within 3 business days and processed via original Stripe payment method.
