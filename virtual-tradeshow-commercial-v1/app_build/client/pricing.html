Frequently Asked Questions & Technical Disclosures

Clear facts on 3D reconstruction feasibility, hardware options, and commercial terms.

Q. How does Photo Immersive Booth differ from 3D Digital Twins?

Photo Immersive uses verified booth photography to construct a smooth interactive panoramic viewer with anchored product pinpoints. True 3D digital twins (Gaussian splatting / photogrammetry) require specialized dense multi-angle camera passes and feasibility qualification under our Custom program.

Q. What are Advanced Product 3D / 360 Media Assets?

These assets include 360° product turntables, multi-angle view sets, and verified 3D models. The BUSINESS plan includes 30 advanced media assets. Additional items can be added as production add-ons.

Q. Is physical Smart Card hardware included?

Digital Smart Card functionality (vCard contact exchange, mobile responsive booth link) is included in all plans. High-durability laser-engraved NFC physical cards and acrylic tabletop stands are available to order separately.

Q. Can I upgrade or request custom enterprise integrations?

Yes. Exhibitors can seamlessly upgrade from PRO to BUSINESS as their product catalog expands, or request a CUSTOM quote for multi-hall trade show circuits and custom CRM webhooks.
