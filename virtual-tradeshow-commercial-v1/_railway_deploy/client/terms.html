1. Purpose and Service Scope

Virtual Trade Show Commercial V1 (the "Platform" or "Company") provides a cloud-based multi-tenant B2B virtual exhibition software solution connecting event organizers, exhibitors, and global buyers. The Platform offers 3D Gaussian Splatting (3DGS) spatial booth reconstruction from multi-angle photographs, interactive product catalogs, digital business card exchanges (Leads), Requests for Quotation (RFQs), and peer-to-peer real-time video consultations.

4. 3D Gaussian Splatting Computational Disclosures

Virtual 3D booths are generated via computer vision algorithms (Structure-from-Motion and neural Gaussian splatting) from multi-view photographs:

  • Reconstruction visual fidelity depends upon capture conditions, photo coverage (recommended 60-80 images), lighting uniformity, and the presence of transparent or reflective surfaces.
  • The Company does not warrant that 3D models constitute a 100% physically identical replication of the physical booth. Exhibitors must verify spatial appearance via Admin Preview prior to public publishing.
