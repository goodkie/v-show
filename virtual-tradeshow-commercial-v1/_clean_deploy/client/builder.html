📸 How to Photograph Your Booth

Option 1: BEST & FASTEST — 360° Camera

Use Insta360, Ricoh Theta, or Matterport. Stand in the center of the booth aisle at eye level (1.5m) and take 1–3 panoramas.

Option 2: ACCESSIBLE — Smartphone / Camera

Take a clear horizontal overview shot from 3–5m away, plus close-ups of major machinery and counters. Generates a crisp Multi-View Showroom.

Option 3: WHITE-GLOVE — Let dn'a Capture It

Our engineering team conducts on-site 8K HDR 360° photography and pinpoint setup for your trade show stand.
