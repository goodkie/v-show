Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q. How does billing and cancellation work?

Paid plans are billed monthly through Stripe's secure infrastructure. You can cancel recurring renewals anytime with a single click inside the Exhibitor Admin Console via the Stripe Customer Portal.

Q. What is 3D Gaussian Splatting (3DGS) reconstruction?

3DGS uses computer vision algorithms to convert 60-80 multi-angle photos into an interactive 3D spatial model (SPZ). It is an algorithmic reconstruction and may differ from physical perfection.

Q. Is my booth data deleted if I cancel my subscription?

No. Your uploaded catalog, company profile, and collected buyer leads are safely preserved and remain accessible within Free tier limits.

Q. What is the refund policy?

Refunds are processed in accordance with our Refund Policy. Once cloud GPU compute resources have been expended on 3DGS reconstruction, partial limitations may apply.
